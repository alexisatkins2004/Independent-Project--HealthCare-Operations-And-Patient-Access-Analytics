# Business Requirements

## Project Overview

Seattle Grace Health Network is a fictional healthcare organization that operates multiple outpatient clinics across the 
United States. Leadership wants to improve operational efficiency, optimize provider utilization, and better understand 
patient experiences through interactive Power BI dashboards.

The objective of this project is to develop an end-to-end analytics solution that transforms raw operational data into 
actionable business insights.

---

# Business Objectives 

- Improve visibility into clinic performance.
- Identify operational bottlenecks affecting patient experience.
- Monitor provider utilization and appointment trends.
- Analyze financial performance across clinics and services.
- Support data-driven decisions making through interactive dashboards.

---

# Business Questions 

1. Which clinics experience the longest patient wait times?

2. What factors contribute to appointment cancellations and no-shows?

3. How effectively is provider capacity being used?

4. Which departments and services generate the strongest margins?

5. How do operational performance and patient satisfaction relate?

6. Which departments are falling below their monthly budgets?

7. How do patient wait times impact satisfaction scores?

8. Which clinics and departments consistently exceed their appointment capacity?

9. How do appointment volumes and revenue change over time?

10. Which clinics demonstrate the best overall operational performance?

---

# Datasets & Descriptions

- Monthly Appoint Files: Individual appointment records for each month.
- Patients: Patient demographic information.
- Providers: Provider information and specialties.
- Clinics: Clinic locations and capacities.
- Services: Medical services and pricing.
- Department Budget: Monthly departmental budgets.

---

# Expected Deliverables

The final Power BI solution will include:

- Clean and validated data
- Star-schema data model
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Executive KPI summary
- Operational analytics
- Financial analytics
- Provider performance analysis
- Documentation of the ETL process

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

- Integrates data from multiple sources.
- Produces a scalable star-schema data model.
- Provides accurate and interactive dashboards.
- Answers each of the identified business questions.
- Demonstrates best practices in Power Query, DAX, and Power BI.

---
